# CallCue

CallCue is a small window that stays on top of your video call. It listens to your computer's sound and shows short suggested replies while you talk, based on your CV.

Works with Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom, in the browser or as desktop apps. Your first session is free.

## How it works

1. Install and open CallCue.
2. Click **Sign in** and sign in with Google in your browser.
3. Join your meeting, choose **Meeting** and click **Start session**.
4. Suggested replies appear in the small window on top of your call.

**Windows:** if Windows says "protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
**Mac:** the first time, right-click CallCue, choose **Open**, then allow **Screen Recording** in System Settings.

CallCue never uses your microphone and never records video. Follow the recording and consent rules that apply to you.
